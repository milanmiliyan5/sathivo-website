Free planning tool · No sign-in required

Plan the time, cost and safety of a platonic meetup.

Before asking someone to join you for coffee, a movie or a public outing, make the practical details clear. This tool estimates the cost of your proposed hourly offer and helps you prepare a safer plan. It runs on this page and does not send your inputs to Sathivo.

By Sathivo · Updated 1 November 2026 · For adults 18+ · Strictly platonic

1. Estimate what your plan could cost

Companions choose their own listed hourly rates. You may offer a different amount in a booking request, but the other person decides whether to accept. An estimate is not an agreement, invoice or reservation. Use the fields below to plan what you might spend.

Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, government IDs, your exact home address or sensitive account details. If someone pressures you to break boundaries, decline and report it.

3. Put the estimate into a real request

Browse the published Sathivo listings. Check which experiences, meeting preferences, city or town and hourly rate each person has chosen to display. Choose a realistic date and time, then send a request. If the companion accepts, use the private booking chat to discuss the exact public venue and agreed price. If no suitable profile is available in your area, you can try another location or check again later; Sathivo does not guarantee availability.

Do not send money merely because someone claims to represent Sathivo. Sathivo currently charges nothing for joining, creating a profile or sending a booking request, and it does not run an in-platform checkout or autopay system.

4. Important limitations

This calculator assumes the agreed hourly offer scales proportionally with duration, but people may negotiate a different arrangement. It does not predict taxes, cancellation arrangements, transport fares, availability, or whether a companion will accept. Do not use it as financial or legal advice.

Sathivo does not verify every person's government identity or age documents. Published profiles are self-described. You must be 18 or older, treat every interaction as strictly platonic, and decide independently whether an arrangement feels right to you.
