# Sathivo editorial roadmap — 21 November 2026

## Status and publication safeguard

This file is a **commissioning and fact-check plan, not 15 published articles**. Google does not mandate a number or word count for AdSense, and producing a large batch of automated near-duplicate pages to satisfy an imagined quota is risky. Editors should publish an article only after verifying every Sathivo feature claim in the current website, checking all links, confirming examples are not presented as testimonials, and finding a real reader problem the article solves.

The current public reading collection is at [Guides](https://sathivo.co/guides.html). This file does not appear in the sitemap. All planned ideas below are **unpublished** and require an identified human reviewer, a factual source check and genuine additional value before release. Authorship must be attributed honestly: do not invent named experts, founder biographies, firsthand tests, quotations, users, cities or reviews. Neither a word-count target nor a publishing date guarantees Google indexation or AdSense approval.

## Each article's publish checklist

- [ ] A human editor has read, corrected and approved the final text.
- [ ] Intro answers a concrete reader question; sections give steps instead of generic filler.
- [ ] Current Sathivo interface/feature statements are reproduced from tested behaviour, not guesswork.
- [ ] Illustrations are labeled as examples; no fabricated reviews, photos, prices, availability or outcomes.
- [ ] No sexual-service encouragement, no identity-verification claim, no blanket refund guarantee.
- [ ] Important safety advice includes public-first meeting, independent transport, consent and no PIN/OTP sharing when relevant.
- [ ] Rates distinguish the companion's own hourly rate, a customer offer and extra costs.
- [ ] Booking transfer and voluntary platform support payment are not conflated.
- [ ] Page has a meaningful title, 1 H1, honest byline, updated date, canonical, internal links and editorial contact.
- [ ] Responsive width, keyboard navigation, link validity and page load are tested on real mobile/desktop.
- [ ] Separate moderation check confirms no prohibited member content embedded in the article.
- [ ] No test ads on blank/error pages; advertising placement stays disabled until the actual publisher account is approved.
- [ ] Only then add it to the guides hub and sitemap and submit for indexing.
