How to choose a companion who fits your plan

These are real, user-published companion listings, not example profiles. Available listings change when members publish, hide or update their profiles. Sathivo does not promise a particular companion will be available on a particular date, in every district or for every activity.

Start with a location or try online

Use the State / UT, District and City / Town filters to search within a chosen area. If your town is not in the popular-city list, select the custom city option and type it. You can also choose an online meeting preference where a companion offers that option. An empty result means no currently published profile matched those filters; it does not mean a booking is available elsewhere.

Compare the actual activity, not just the picture

Check the companion's written bio, listed activities, languages, meeting preference and usual availability. Published details are self-described. Sathivo does not guarantee government-ID verification, a match, personal compatibility or immediate replies. Never treat a profile photo or rating as proof of identity.

Understand the price before you request

Each companion sets their own hourly rate. When sending a request, you can propose an hourly offer and duration. Only if the companion accepts and both people agree to a plan should you treat the amount as agreed. Sathivo currently has no platform fee, automatic debit or in-platform payment gateway. Travel, food and tickets should be discussed separately. Use the free meetup budget planner and safety checklist to estimate a realistic total.

Plan with respect and clear boundaries

Sathivo is only for adults aged 18 or older and strictly platonic social experiences. Agree on the activity, boundaries and a public place before an in-person first meeting. Do not send OTPs, UPI PINs or sensitive personal information. Read the first-meeting safety checklist and the booking and payment guide before booking. Report suspicious behaviour through Support.
