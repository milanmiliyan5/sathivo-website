IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Understand the limits before you plan.

This disclaimer explains what information on Sathivo can and cannot establish. It supplements, and does not replace, our Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines.

General information, not professional advice

Our guides, articles, budgeting tools and examples are provided for general informational purposes. They are not legal, medical, financial, emergency or mental-health advice. Cost examples use stated assumptions and do not guarantee actual prices, availability or outcomes. Consult qualified professionals where the matter requires specialist advice.

Information supplied by members

Companion listings, profile descriptions, availability statements and communications are supplied or changed by users. A profile may be incomplete or become outdated. Sathivo does not guarantee the accuracy of user-supplied claims, someone’s identity, age verification, personal compatibility, conduct or willingness to accept a request. An email-verification indicator is not government-ID verification.

Requests are not confirmed until accepted

Sending a booking request does not guarantee a response or meeting. A companion may accept, decline or cancel depending on the available controls. An accepted request still requires participants to agree on exact arrangements and boundaries; neither an acceptance indicator nor a completed booking establishes that payment occurred. See booking status help.

Companion booking payments versus optional support

In the current Sathivo model, companions set their own hourly rates; customers may offer a different rate. Sathivo does not take, hold, settle or refund payments between companions and customers. Any lawful payment is arranged directly between those participants. Disputes about off-platform transfers cannot automatically be resolved or refunded by Sathivo. The separate Support Sathivo page uses Razorpay for optional, one-time platform contributions. A contribution does not pay a companion, reserve a booking, unlock priority treatment or provide a guarantee of service.

Personal safety, consent and reports

Sathivo is strictly platonic and only for adults aged 18 or above. Members remain responsible for their own decisions, consent, meeting location, transportation and handling of private information. Choose public places for first meetings, share plans with someone you trust, respect a refusal, and do not share OTPs, UPI PINs or passwords. Reporting and moderation tools can help raise concerns, but they cannot guarantee prevention of misconduct or immediate intervention. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

External services, links and changes

External services such as payment apps, maps, ticket vendors and other links are operated independently and may apply their own policies. Sathivo's pages may change to reflect new functions, policy updates or corrections. Check the latest guide or contact Sathivo if something seems inconsistent.
