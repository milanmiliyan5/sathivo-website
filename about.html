ABOUT SATHIVO

Shared activities.
Clear expectations.

Sathivo helps adults in India discover people offering strictly platonic conversation and shared social activities. This page explains what the platform provides, what you agree with another member, and where to get help.

What you can use Sathivo for

Browse published profiles for conversation, coffee, movies, shopping, events, walks, online chat or phone conversation. A listing shows the activities a companion chooses to offer, their location, languages, usual availability and hourly rate. Choose an activity and meeting preference that the profile actually supports.

Sathivo is for people aged 18 or older. All activities must remain platonic. Members must respect consent, personal boundaries and the community guidelines.

From a profile to a shared plan

  1. Use Explore to compare public profiles and filter by location, activity or meeting preference. Availability depends on members who have published in that area.
  2. Sign in and send a request with your chosen activity, proposed time, duration and hourly offer. The companion decides whether to accept.
  3. After acceptance, use the booking chat to agree on the meeting details, total cost and boundaries. Discuss any changes before proceeding.

Sending a request does not guarantee acceptance or an immediate reply. See the booking guide for request statuses and hourly-offer examples.

Rates, costs and payments

Each companion sets their own hourly rate. Sathivo currently charges no platform fee and does not collect or process booking payments. Members agree on payment directly. Clarify the accepted rate, duration, travel, food and ticket costs before meeting. The meetup budget planner helps estimate these separate costs.

If a plan changes or someone cancels, discuss any payment or refund directly with the other member. Report suspected fraud through Support. Sathivo cannot promise recovery of money transferred outside the platform.

What a published profile tells you

Profiles contain details supplied by members. Publication means the member chose to make the listing public. A photo, avatar, verified email or rating does not establish someone's identity or guarantee their conduct. Government-ID verification is not currently provided.

Companions can edit or hide their listings. Read the bio, activities and availability and ask questions before deciding. If you publish a profile, use your own accurate information and keep it current. Our profile guide explains how to describe your offering without sharing private contact details.

Safety, reports and moderation

For a first in-person meeting, choose a public venue, tell someone you trust and arrange your own transport. Keep passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs and sensitive documents private. The first-meeting checklist helps you prepare.

Use the available report or block controls when needed, or select Safety report on the Support page. Include the relevant profile or booking reference and describe what happened. Authorized Sathivo admins can review reports and restrict or remove content or accounts under the community rules. Reporting does not guarantee an immediate outcome. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Privacy and account control

Companion details become public when you publish. Your login credentials and private support messages are not part of the public listing. Read the Privacy Policy for data use and retention details. You can hide your companion listing or use Delete account when you want to leave.

Contact Sathivo

For account access, technical issues, privacy questions or a safety concern, use Sathivo Support or email sathivoofficial@gmail.com. Include the relevant page and a description of the issue. Never include passwords or OTPs. Send suggestions through Feedback.

Our public guides and tools explain the service's current behaviour. If something is inaccurate or out of date, contact Support with the page link so it can be checked.
