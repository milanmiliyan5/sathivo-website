From a profile to a plan: how a Sathivo booking works

You should know the price, what you are arranging, and what happens after you send a request. This guide explains Sathivo's current booking model with examples—without promising that someone will be available in your area.

By Sathivo · Updated 14 October 2026 · For adults 18+ · Strictly platonic

1. Begin with the activity, not just the profile picture

Sathivo is an adults-only platform for strictly platonic company: a coffee conversation, public walk, movie outing, shopping trip, online chat or another ordinary social plan. It is not a dating or escort service. Start with the kind of experience you want, whether you would prefer to meet online or in person, and the amount of time you can realistically spend.

Use Find company to browse published profiles. The directory includes filters for state, district, city or town, activity and meeting preference. A profile may show interests, languages and an hourly rate. Availability depends on the actual people who have chosen to publish profiles; Sathivo does not guarantee a match in any town.

2. Understand the listed rate and your own offer

Companions set their own hourly rates. Sathivo does not decide those prices. When you send a booking request, you may offer a different hourly amount. The person receiving the request can accept or decline it. An accepted offer becomes the agreed hourly rate recorded for that booking.

Illustrative example—not a real listing

  • A companion lists ₹300 per hour.
  • You request a one-hour coffee conversation and offer ₹250 per hour.
  • If the companion accepts, the accepted hourly offer is ₹250. If they do not accept, there is no agreed booking at that amount.

Before meeting, clarify any transport, food, tickets or extra time directly. Do not assume these are included in a listed hourly rate.

Sending a request is free in Sathivo's current launch model. The platform does not collect a booking fee or commission at this stage.

3. What to include in your request

A specific plan is easier to consider than a vague message. Choose an activity and propose a realistic date, time, meeting preference and hourly offer using the booking form. Make sure the amount you offer is one you are comfortable paying if accepted.

A good plan sounds like this

“Coffee and conversation, Saturday afternoon, one hour, at a public café in the city centre. I can offer ₹250 per hour. We can confirm the venue in chat if the request is accepted.”

This is a planning example, not a message sent by a real customer.

Do not put passwords, personal ID scans, your full home address or payment PINs into a request or chat. Only disclose details required for a safe arrangement.

4. After you send: pending, accepted or declined

A booking request is not an automatic confirmation. The other person must decide whether to accept it. You can follow the request's status in Sathivo's request or booking screens. A request may stay pending, be accepted, be declined or be cancelled depending on the user's decision and the available controls.

Accepted-booking chat helps the participants discuss the plan. Confirm the exact activity, expected duration, public location if meeting in person, arrival time, individual travel arrangements and any boundaries. If those details change, ask again rather than assuming prior consent carries over.

Do not travel to a meeting or make a payment merely because you sent a request. Wait until the plan has been clearly agreed by both people.

5. How payments work in the current version

Sathivo does not accept, hold or transfer booking money. If a customer and companion agree to a payment, they arrange it directly using a lawful method such as UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe or cash. Sathivo cannot reverse an off-platform transfer, act as escrow or guarantee a refund.

  • Double-check the agreed hourly rate and the duration before paying.
  • Be wary of demands for large advance transfers or money to a third party.
  • Never share your UPI PIN, bank OTP, card PIN, password or screen-sharing access.
  • Keep a clear record of what the two of you agreed, without publishing anyone's private details.

If you are uncomfortable with the terms or feel pressured, do not proceed. Read the full Terms of Use for the platform's actual payment boundaries.

6. If plans change or you need to cancel

Plans sometimes fall through. Use the available cancellation controls when appropriate, and tell the other participant promptly when you can. Because booking payments happen outside Sathivo, any payment dispute or refund request must be addressed directly between the two participants; the platform does not process refunds. Avoid making assumptions about cancellation charges that neither side explicitly agreed to.

If someone asks for a prohibited service, becomes aggressive, misrepresents their intention or pressures you for personal information, stop engaging and use the website's reporting or Support options.

7. Once the experience ends

Keep things respectful at the end of the agreed time. Continuing for longer should be a new, mutual decision—not an automatic extension. If the website offers a review for a completed booking, describe the actual experience fairly without revealing private contact details or making unverified allegations.

For a first in-person outing, see the first-meeting safety checklist. If you are publishing your own services, the companion profile guide explains how to set expectations clearly.

8. Quick answers

Does Sathivo choose my price?

No. Companions set their listed hourly rate and customers can submit their own hourly offer.

Can a booking be guaranteed?

No. A companion has to accept a request and both people must still agree on the plan.

Is this a dating service?

No. All activities must remain legal, non-sexual and strictly platonic. Both customers and companions must be at least 18.

Does Sathivo hold the money?

No. Payment arrangements are outside the platform in the current version.
