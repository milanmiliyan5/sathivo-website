How it works · Useful public guidance

How Sathivo works: from browsing to a clear, platonic plan

A practical explanation of the actual website workflow, what the profile badges mean, what happens after acceptance and why a booking is not an automatic payment.

By Sathivo · Updated 21 November 2026 · Adults 18+ · Strictly platonic

1. Who Sathivo is for—and what it is not

Sathivo is a platform for adults aged 18 and older who want strictly platonic company for ordinary activities: conversation, coffee, shopping, a movie, a walk in a public place or an online discussion. People might want someone to share an interest with, explore a public event or practise having an easy conversation. This is not a dating service, an escort marketplace or a place to request romance or sexual services. Both sides must respect boundaries and can choose not to proceed.

A listed profile is a member's description of the activities they are willing to offer. Some companions set hourly rates for their time. The platform helps people discover profiles, make requests and coordinate after acceptance. It does not guarantee that anyone will respond, accept a request, appear at a venue or be available in a particular city.

2. Browse public profiles and understand the indicators

Start at Explore. Filter by state, district, city or town, experience and meeting preference. Look for an activity you genuinely enjoy rather than picking a person from a photo alone. Read the bio, listed language, rate, preferences and stated availability. If a city has no suitable published listings, change your filters or consider an online option where a member has offered one. Do not assume the platform has someone in every district.

Important verification distinction: members may display an email-verified indicator and self-declared age or accepted platonic boundaries. Those are not government identity checks or proof that a person is trustworthy. Sathivo does not currently claim to verify every member's identity or personally approve their behaviour. A profile photo, rating or publication status is never a safety guarantee. Use common sense and the community rules.

3. Send a clear request that someone can answer

After finding a suitable public listing, sign in to make a booking request. Name the activity, a realistic time and duration, meeting preference and proposed hourly rate. Sathivo companions set their own displayed hourly rates, while a customer may propose another amount. The companion chooses whether to accept or decline the offer. Sending a request does not automatically reserve time or create a payment obligation to Sathivo.

Illustrative request—not a real booking

“I would like a one-hour coffee conversation on Saturday afternoon, at a public café close to the city centre. I can offer ₹250 per hour. Please let me know if that activity and time work for you; we can agree on a specific meeting point after acceptance.”

This example is deliberately specific about time, activity and rate without publishing a home address or demanding personal contact information. Do not use it to assume a ₹250 offer will be accepted.

4. Acceptance, chat and confirming the actual plan

Open My Bookings to see the status of your own request. A pending request is not a confirmed meeting. If accepted, the booking's conversation provides a place to agree on the exact activity, duration, venue or online format, what each person will cover and any boundaries. If the recipient declines, respect the decision rather than sending repeated requests. Two separate bookings with the same person can have separate chat threads; check the matching activity and date.

Discuss changes before acting. For example, if an accepted one-hour plan becomes a 90-minute plan, both participants should agree on the new time and any price difference. A chat message does not override consent. You can still decide that the arrangement is not right for you. See the status and cancellation guide for detailed next steps.

5. Plan a safer first public meeting

Choose a familiar public venue, such as a café, restaurant, library common area or public event with other people nearby. Confirm the entrance and an end time, consider daylight where practical, arrange your own return transport and tell someone you trust about the plan. A first meet in a secluded room or private residence is a poor choice. Both participants should be able to leave independently. Sathivo recommends public first meetings and clear personal boundaries; it cannot remotely supervise a meetup or promise that a location or person is safe.

If someone pressures you to change the venue, share private documents, turn on a camera, extend the meeting or ignore a stated boundary, you can refuse or stop. Report suspicious behaviour through available profile reporting tools or Support. Consult the safety checklist before heading out.

6. How money works: no Sathivo booking payment gateway

Companion rates are set by companions, not by Sathivo. Discuss the offered rate and duration, and separately agree on tickets, food and travel. In the current service, booking payments happen directly between the two users outside Sathivo, using their own lawful arrangements such as UPI or cash. Sathivo does not take a companion-booking commission, debit a customer automatically, hold money in escrow or refund transfers made directly to another user.

The Support Sathivo Razorpay checkout is different: it accepts optional one-time contributions to support the platform, and does not pay a companion, reserve a booking or create an entitlement to a service. If someone claims Sathivo is automatically collecting a booking deposit, confirm through official Contact before sending any funds. Never share UPI PINs, OTPs, passwords or screen access.

7. Cancelling, finishing, reviewing and reporting

If you cannot attend, use the cancellation control available for that booking and communicate respectfully where appropriate. You may see statuses such as Pending, Accepted, Declined, Cancelled and Completed. These describe the state of a request, not whether an off-platform transfer succeeded or an experience was satisfactory. Do not assume Sathivo can reverse a payment arranged directly between participants. Use booking help if a status is confusing or contact Support with a booking reference and factual description.

Reviews, if available for the completed booking, should describe an actual experience fairly, without private phone numbers, allegations presented as fact or false testimonials. Harassment, pressure for sexual services and impersonation are incompatible with the community rules. Reports are reviewed through moderation processes; a report does not automatically mean immediate suspension or guarantee a particular outcome.

8. Frequently asked questions

Can I browse without signing in?

Published companion listings and public guides are designed to be readable without an account. Sending a booking request and accessing private member features require sign-in.

Are companions fully verified?

No. Email verification or self-declared information is not a full identity or background check. Never interpret profile badges as a guarantee.

Is Sathivo free?

Joining, profile publishing and sending requests are currently free. An individual companion may charge an agreed hourly amount, arranged and paid directly between users. Optional support contributions are separate.

Is a first-time meeting required to be public?

The platform strongly recommends public venues for in-person first meetings; participants must follow the published rules and make their own informed arrangements. It does not promise on-site monitoring.

Can I cancel after someone accepts?

Use the controls available for that booking and communicate respectfully. Any off-platform payments or disputes are not automatically reversed by the website.

What if I feel unsafe?

End the interaction when safe, use report or block features, and contact local emergency services if there is immediate danger. Sathivo support is not an emergency response service.
