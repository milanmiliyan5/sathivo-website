Account privacy & community boundaries

Sathivo is for adults 18+ and strictly platonic companionship. Sexual services, sexual offers, escort services and sexual activity are prohibited. Respect consent, privacy and boundaries.

When you create an account, your name, email address and declarations are stored with our authentication provider, Supabase. Your password is handled by Supabase Auth; Sathivo's page does not save it. Verification and recovery emails are sent through Resend to the address you provide.

Sign-in session tokens are stored in this browser so you can stay signed in. Password reset sessions stay in memory and end when you leave or reload this page. Sign out when using a shared device.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Google Fonts supplies the fonts. These providers receive normal connection information such as IP addresses. This release has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Account testing is in progress. Companion profiles, bookings and payments are not available yet. The full service privacy policy, support contact and account deletion process are still being prepared.
