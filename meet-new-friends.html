A thoughtful way to find company

Whether you searched for “meet new friends”, “friendship app India”, or “find friends near me”, Sathivo works differently from a social feed or a dating app. Companions publish profiles and set their own hourly rates. You can browse available listings and send a booking request with an hourly offer that works for you.

Creating an account, publishing a profile and sending a booking request are free on Sathivo. The companion decides whether to accept. After an accepted request, both people can use booking chat to agree on the activity, meeting location and boundaries. Any payment is handled directly between the users; Sathivo does not collect it.

Questions about finding friends

Is Sathivo a dating, relationship or couples-dating app?

No. Sathivo is strictly for platonic companionship and shared social activities. It does not offer romance, dating, couple-to-couple matching, escort or sexual services. Adults, including couples, may explore appropriate platonic outings where a companion agrees.

Is meeting new friends on Sathivo free?

Browsing, signup, companion profiles and sending a booking request are free. Individual companions set their own hourly prices for their time. You can propose a different hourly amount in a request.

Can I find a companion near me?

Search available profiles for your area. In-person availability depends entirely on companions who have joined and published listings near you; no specific city or match is guaranteed.

How do I meet safely?

Meet in a public place, tell someone you trust about your plans, keep control of your transport and never share sensitive passwords or payment PINs. Read our Safety & Community Guidelines.
