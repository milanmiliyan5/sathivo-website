What is Sathivo?+

Sathivo is an adults-only platform for strictly platonic companionship, conversations and shared social activities. Creating an account and profile is free. Companions set their own hourly companionship rates, and customers can send a different hourly offer.

Is this a dating or escort platform?+

No. Sathivo is strictly for platonic companionship. Dating services, escort services, sexual services, sexual offers, and sexual activity are not allowed on the platform.

Can I book a companion right now?+

Yes. Browse a published profile, see the companion’s hourly rate, and send a booking request with your own hourly offer. If the companion accepts, that offer becomes the agreed rate. Sathivo does not collect or process the payment.

Can I earn as a companion on Sathivo?+

Yes. Adults can create a companion profile for free, set their own hourly companionship rate, publish the profile and choose which booking requests to accept. Earnings depend on accepted bookings, and payment is settled directly between users outside Sathivo.

Who can use Sathivo?+

Only people aged 18 or older who agree to our platonic-only rules and respect other people's boundaries. This applies to both customers and companions.

Does Sathivo charge for booking requests?+

Sathivo does not currently charge a platform booking fee. A companion may set their own hourly rate. The customer can propose a different amount, and an accepted offer becomes the agreed rate. Any payment is settled directly between the two users outside Sathivo.

Will Sathivo be available in my city?+

Sathivo supports India-wide location browsing and online experiences. In-person availability depends on companions who have published profiles in your area; we cannot promise a match in every town.

Is a conversation a mental health service?+

No. A companion can offer everyday conversation and a listening ear, but companionship is not therapy, counselling, or crisis support. Contact a qualified professional for mental health care, and local emergency services if you are in immediate danger.
