LET'S CLEAR A FEW THINGS UP

Good questions.
Honest answers.

A little clarity before you connect.

What is Sathivo?+

Sathivo is a platform in development for adults to find paid, strictly platonic companionship for conversations and shared social activities. Companions will create their own profiles and set their own rates.

Is this a dating or escort platform?+

No. Sathivo is strictly for platonic companionship. Dating services, escort services, sexual services, sexual offers, and sexual activity are not allowed on the platform.

Can I book a companion right now?+

Not yet. This is our first website release. You can explore planned experiences and our community expectations. Live profiles, applications, and bookings are coming later.

Who can use Sathivo?+

Only people aged 18 or older who agree to our platonic-only rules and respect other people's boundaries. This applies to both customers and companions.

How much will it cost?+

Companions will set their own rates. Platform fees and payment arrangements are not final yet. All applicable charges will need to be shown before a booking is confirmed.

Will Sathivo be available in my city?+

We are planning for India-wide access, including online experiences. In-person availability will depend on companions joining in your area. No cities or companion availability are confirmed yet.

Is a conversation a mental health service?+

No. A companion can offer everyday conversation and a listening ear, but companionship is not therapy, counselling, or crisis support. Contact a qualified professional for mental health care, and local emergency services if you are in immediate danger.
