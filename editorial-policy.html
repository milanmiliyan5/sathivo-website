OUR PUBLIC INFORMATION

Clear information. Clear limits.

Sathivo publishes guidance to help adults understand the website's actual features, the direct-payment model and safer platonic plans. This page explains how to evaluate a guide and where to report inaccuracies.

Who is named as the publisher?

Unless an article specifically identifies a different individual, the publishing name “Sathivo” refers to the platform's own informational pages, not a separately certified author or a named professional expert. We do not attribute articles to invented writers, invent qualifications, or claim that user examples are real testimonials. The public contact for this content is Contact Sathivo.

What these guides cover

Our guide collection describes current website features, how user-set rates and offered rates differ, booking statuses, direct transfers outside the platform, profile privacy and practical meeting precautions. When an example includes a price, plan or message, it is illustrative unless clearly and verifiably described otherwise. A hypothetical should never be mistaken for a completed booking or available companion.

How to read dates and limitations

A page's “Updated” date describes the article's content, not the last time an individual companion was available. Live listings can change at any moment. A guide is not a safety endorsement of an individual or verification that a listed person will respond. Individual medical, legal, financial and emergency questions should go to a qualified professional or the relevant service.

Review and source standards

We aim to compare feature explanations with the live Sathivo website and its published Terms, Privacy Policy and Safety rules; remove inaccurate, repetitive or obsolete instructions; and distinguish guidance from guarantees. Research and drafting assistance may be used, but software-assisted writing is not a substitute for factual review or real user evidence. We do not present invented testing or credentials as a sign of expertise. New articles should be published only when their feature details and links can be checked.

Corrections and member content

If a guide describes an outdated button, omits an important step or makes an incorrect claim, send the page URL, the problematic text and a factual correction through Support or sathivoofficial@gmail.com. Separate from platform-written guides, public companion profiles are user-submitted content. Inappropriate, misleading or sexually suggestive listings should be reported through the relevant profile or safety channel; they should not be quoted as platform endorsements.

What we do not promise

No number of articles ensures Google indexing, search rankings, advertising approval, member identity or booking outcomes. Pages are intended first for people trying to use the website. For the service's legal limitations, read our Disclaimer and Terms.
