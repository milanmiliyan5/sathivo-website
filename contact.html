What to include in a useful report

For a technical error, include the page URL, device/browser, what you expected, and the visible error message. For a booking concern, include the relevant booking reference and what happened without revealing the other person's private details unnecessarily. For potentially abusive profiles, use the in-product report control when possible and explain the concern factually.

Sathivo does not promise an instant answer, a fixed support time or recovery of an off-platform payment. If somebody appears to be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than waiting for a website response.

Contact boundaries and important links

Sathivo coordinates strictly platonic interactions for adults aged 18 or older. Companion payments are arranged directly between participants, not collected by the platform. Optional platform-support contributions are separate. Read the Terms, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, and Safety rules for more context.
