    About rankings

    Most Active uses a 30-day activity score starting at 100 points. Genuine activity days, responses, accepted requests, two-way conversations, completed bookings and positive reviews earn points. Repeated messages and refreshes do not earn extra points; repeat activity with the same person is capped. Most Booked counts completed bookings. Top Rated requires at least two completed-booking reviews. Rankings are not an endorsement or a guarantee of availability.
