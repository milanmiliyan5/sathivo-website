Practical help · No sign-in needed to read

A clear next step for every booking status.

You sent a request, changed your mind or cannot find the chat. Use this guide to understand what the status really means, what you can do next, and when to contact Sathivo support. It describes Sathivo's current website workflow, not a guarantee that a companion will respond.

By Sathivo · Updated 21 November 2026 · Adults 18+ · Strictly platonic

1. Find your booking's next step

Sign in and open My Bookings or Incoming Requests, depending on your role. Locate the correct request by activity, time and person. An accepted booking may have its own conversation in Chats; requests for different activities with the same person can lead to different booking threads.

Pending: the companion has not accepted the request. Do not treat it as a confirmed plan or send an advance payment solely because the request was submitted. This tool gives general guidance only. It does not read your account, change a booking, contact anyone or submit a request.
StatusWhat it means and what to do
PendingThe companion has not accepted or declined yet. Wait for a decision. If your schedule changes, use the available cancellation control rather than making a second identical request.
AcceptedThe companion accepted the request. Use that booking's chat to confirm time, location or online format, duration, boundary expectations and any extra costs. Acceptance is not proof of payment.
DeclinedThat proposed plan was not accepted. Respect the decision. You can browse other published listings or plan for another time, without sending repeated pressure messages.
CancelledThe request was cancelled. Check any displayed reason and contact the other person only if appropriate. Sathivo does not automatically issue refunds for money transferred outside the platform.
CompletedThe booking has been marked finished. This status alone does not confirm the actual length, payment or satisfaction. If it was marked incorrectly, use support or the available booking-correction flow.

2. Check the price, time and activity before you agree

A companion sets a displayed hourly rate. A customer can send a different hourly offer. The amount to use in your plan is the offer accepted for that booking, not automatically the first rate you saw. Agree on the duration and who pays for tickets, food or transport before meeting.

Example: changing a 90-minute coffee plan

A companion lists ₹300 an hour; you offer ₹250 an hour for 90 minutes. If they accept that offer, the time estimate is ₹375 (₹250 × 1.5 hours), before separately agreed expenses. If you later want two hours, do not assume the original plan or total automatically extends. Ask for agreement on the changed duration and amount.

This is an illustrative example, not a real user's booking, a guaranteed rate or a charge collected by Sathivo. Use the free cost calculator to compare your own options.

Sathivo is currently free to join, publish a profile and send booking requests. It does not collect, hold or settle companion booking payments. An off-platform payment might be made through the participants' own lawful arrangements; the platform cannot reverse such transfers or promise a refund.

3. When a plan needs cancelling

  1. Open the correct booking. Match its activity, date, and other participant; do not cancel a different request with a similar name.
  2. Use the available Cancel action and give an accurate reason. If the control is not available for that status, do not assume editing a message cancels the booking.
  3. Notify the other person politely if relevant. An accepted plan can involve travel or ticket arrangements. A clear message helps avoid misunderstanding, but never puts you under an obligation to continue an unsafe interaction.
  4. Keep payment discussions separate from Sathivo. If either person transferred money directly, agree on any lawful repayment between yourselves. Sathivo does not operate a payment gateway, automatic debit, escrow or automatic refund for companion bookings.
  5. Report fraud, harassment or pressure. Use Support or the available user-report/block tools. Do not share another person's phone number, banking credentials or documents publicly.

There is no blanket Sathivo cancellation fee established by sending a request. Do not assume an off-platform refund or charge without reviewing the actual agreement between the participants. Read the Terms for the current platform boundaries.

4. Common problems and how to resolve them

I sent a request, but cannot see a chat.

Check the request's status in My Bookings. Booking chat is associated with an accepted request; while pending, the other person still has to decide. Check the correct booking before sending a duplicate.

Someone has not replied.

Users choose whether and when to accept requests. A pending request is not a confirmed meeting, and availability shown on a profile is a preference, not a guaranteed appointment. Avoid advance travel commitments.

The date or amount is wrong.

Before confirmation, review the selected time, duration and hourly offer. If a request was already sent, use available cancellation or discussion controls rather than assuming you can silently change a previously agreed plan.

A person changed the venue to somewhere private.

You can decline a change that feels unsafe. Prefer a familiar public location for a first in-person meeting, make your own transport plan, and report pressure or prohibited requests.

I was told to pay Sathivo automatically.

Sathivo does not operate autopay or collect companion booking fees. Verify who is requesting funds. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or remote-screen access.

The page won't load on my phone.

Check your connection, reload once, and try the direct Bookings page. If the issue persists, report the page URL, approximate time and an error screenshot via Support, without sharing login codes.

5. Safety reports and urgent situations

Use Sathivo's reporting and blocking controls for suspicious requests, harassment, coercion, impersonation or a request for sexual services. All interactions must remain strictly platonic and are limited to adults aged 18 or above. Reports may be reviewed by authorised moderators; submitting a report does not guarantee a particular outcome or immediate response.

When sending Support a concern, include a booking reference, a short factual description and the approximate time. Share only information you are comfortable disclosing. Do not upload public accusations or sensitive identity documents. If you are in immediate danger, seek local help and contact emergency services; in India, 112 is the emergency response number.

For practical in-person and online precautions, see the first-meeting checklist and community guidelines.

6. Quick questions

Does clicking Accept mean payment happened?

No. Acceptance changes the booking status. Sathivo does not process companion booking payments, so an accepted request is not evidence of a transfer.

Can I change my mind after accepting?

People can withdraw from an arrangement. Use the controls available for that booking and communicate appropriately, without compromising your safety.

Will the platform automatically refund me?

No. Sathivo has no companion-booking refund or escrow system. Discuss off-platform transfers directly with the other participant and seek appropriate help if fraud is suspected.

Can I book without a suitable companion in my city?

No particular profile, availability or acceptance is guaranteed. Try changing filters, consider an online option where listed, or check again later. Do not invent or assume local availability.

Does booking chat make a meeting safe?

No. Profiles, avatars, verified email and messages are not identity checks. Read the safety tips, respect boundaries and choose a public first meeting.
